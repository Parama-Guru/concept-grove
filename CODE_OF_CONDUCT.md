# Code of conduct

Concept Grove is a place to learn and build together. This short, original policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces.

## Treat people with care

- Welcome different backgrounds, experience levels, and ways of learning.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Explain disagreements with evidence and leave room for correction.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, or target others with personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and authorship. Do not publish another person's private information or material you lack permission to share.
- Keep discussions relevant and constructive; avoid spam and repeated unwanted contact.

## Raise a concern

Report conduct concerns privately to [paramaguruvh@gmail.com](mailto:paramaguruvh@gmail.com). Include the relevant links and only the personal information needed to understand the concern. Do not post private reports in public issues.

Maintainers consider context and impact and may request changes, remove content, or restrict participation. Reports should be shared only as needed to address the concern; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised. If a moderation decision seems mistaken, provide additional context through the same private channel for reconsideration.

For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
